chance is 0.333. Two things to read:
  * W_rec=zero rows are NOT expected at chance here, and that is why
    this form is worth running: tanh(W_in u) already contains the
    cubic cross-term the task asks for, so an uncoupled bank is not a
    filter bank. The control that falsifies under --drive output does
    not exist under --drive input.
  * `leverage` is how much zeroing W_rec changes the logits. Below
    ~1e-2 the recurrence variable does nothing and any sweep over it
    is measuring noise, however many seeds it averages.
